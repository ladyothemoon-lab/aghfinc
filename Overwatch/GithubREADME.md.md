# AgroHouse Foundation (AGHF Inc.)

Welcome to the official documents repository for **AgroHouse Foundation (AGHF Inc.)**, a Florida nonprofit corporation.

## About Us

AgroHouse Foundation is a Florida-based nonprofit organization committed to strengthening communities through outreach, stabilization, and support programs. AGHF Inc. has completed its Articles of Incorporation and Florida charitable solicitation registration, and its application for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status is currently in progress.

* **Legal Name:** AGHF Inc.
* **DBA:** AgroHouse Foundation
* **State of Incorporation:** Florida
* **EIN:** 42-4075687
* **Tax-Exempt Status:** 501(c)(3) determination pending

## What's in This Repository

This repository serves as a public record of AGHF's governance, program, and organizational documents, organized into the following categories:

1. **Governance \& Corporate Records** — Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, board resolutions, and policies
2. **Regulatory \& Compliance** — State and federal filings, registrations, and compliance documentation
3. **Program Approval Packages** — Charters, operating policies, budgets, and materials for AGHF's active programs
4. **Fundraising, Grants \& Donations** — Fundraising plans and grant-related materials
5. **Partnerships \& Community Development** — Memoranda of understanding and partnership agreements
6. **Financial Planning \& Operations** — Budgets and financial policies
7. **Marketing \& Communications** — Public-facing materials and communications resources
8. **Strategic Development** — Long-term planning documents
