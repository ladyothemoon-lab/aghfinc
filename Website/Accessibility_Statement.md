# Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

AgroHouse Foundation is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities, including people with disabilities. We are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website, www.AgroHousefoundation.org, and to provide an inclusive experience for all visitors.

---

## 1. Our Commitment

We strive to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**, which is the widely recognized standard for web accessibility. These guidelines help make web content more accessible for people with a range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

---

## 2. Accessibility Features

We aim to design and maintain our website with the following in mind:
- Clear, consistent navigation and page structure
- Text alternatives (alt text) for meaningful images
- Sufficient color contrast between text and background
- Resizable text that does not break the layout
- Keyboard-navigable menus, forms, and interactive elements
- Descriptive link text and form labels
- Compatibility with common screen reader software

---

## 3. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We periodically review our website content and design, and we work to address issues as they are identified. As we add new content, features, or pages, we aim to apply these same accessibility standards.

---

## 4. Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some parts of our website may not yet be fully accessible. This may include older documents, embedded third-party content (such as maps, videos, or donation widgets), or newly added pages that have not yet been reviewed. We are actively working to identify and correct these issues.

---

## 5. Third-Party Content

Our website may include content or tools provided by third parties (for example, payment processors, social media feeds, or embedded videos). We do not control the accessibility of third-party content and encourage those providers to meet accessibility standards independently.

---

## 6. Feedback and Assistance

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, have difficulty accessing any content, or need information in an alternative format, please contact us:

**AgroHouse Foundation**
Email: Info@AgroHousefoundation.org
Website: www.AgroHousefoundation.org

Please include the web page or feature you had trouble accessing, and, if possible, the assistive technology or browser you were using. We will do our best to respond promptly and provide the information, item, or service you are seeking through an accessible alternative.

---

## 7. Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to file a complaint with the relevant regulatory body in your jurisdiction (for example, the U.S. Department of Justice for matters related to the Americans with Disabilities Act, where applicable).

---
