# Donation Policy

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

AgroHouse Foundation is deeply grateful for the generosity of our donors. This Donation Policy explains how we accept, process, acknowledge, and use donations, and outlines donor rights.

---

## 1. Accepting Donations

AgroHouse Foundation accepts donations through our website, by mail, and at events, in the form of one-time gifts, recurring gifts, and, where applicable, in-kind donations. Donations may be made by credit/debit card, bank transfer, or other payment methods offered on our website, processed securely through a third-party payment processor.

We reserve the right to decline any donation that we determine, in our sole discretion, is inconsistent with our mission, values, or legal obligations.

## 3. Donation Receipts

- Donors will receive an email confirmation and receipt for donations made through our website.
- For donations of $250 or more (or the applicable threshold under local law), we will provide a written acknowledgment suitable for tax purposes, including the amount donated and a statement of whether any goods or services were provided in exchange.
- If a donor receives goods or services in exchange for a donation (e.g., an event ticket, merchandise), only the portion of the donation exceeding the fair market value of those goods or services may be tax-deductible, and this will be noted on the receipt where applicable.

---

## 4. Use of Donations

- **Unrestricted donations** are used at the discretion of AgroHouse Foundation to support our general operations and programs.
- **Restricted donations** (designated by the donor for a specific program, project, or purpose) will be used as designated, to the extent practicable. If a designated program is no longer active or fully funded, we reserve the right to redirect the donation to a similar program consistent with the donor's original intent.
- Administrative and fundraising costs may be covered by donations unless a donor specifies otherwise, consistent with standard nonprofit practice.

---

## 5. Recurring Donations

Donors who set up recurring (monthly or periodic) donations may modify or cancel their recurring donation at any time by contacting us at Info@AgroHousefoundation.org or through the donor account/payment portal, where available. Changes may take a short time to process and may not apply to a payment already in progress.

---

## 6. Refund Policy

Donations are generally non-refundable, as they are given voluntarily and without expectation of goods or services in return. However, we recognize that errors can happen. If you believe a donation was made in error (e.g., duplicate charge, incorrect amount), please contact us within [Insert timeframe, e.g., 30 days] at Info@AgroHousefoundation.org, and we will review the request in good faith on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 7. Donor Privacy

- We do not sell, rent, or trade donor information to third parties.
- Donor information is collected and used in accordance with our **Privacy Policy**.
- Donors may request to remain anonymous in any public materials (e.g., annual reports, donor walls, or press releases) by notifying us at the time of donation or afterward at Info@AgroHousefoundation.org.

---

## 8. In-Kind and Non-Cash Donations

For donations of goods, services, or other non-cash items, AgroHouse Foundation will provide a written acknowledgment describing the donated item(s), but will not assign a monetary value to the donation. Donors are responsible for determining the fair market value of in-kind gifts for tax purposes, in consultation with a tax professional.

---

## 9. Donor Rights

As a donor, you have the right to:
- Know how your donation will be used
- Receive prompt, truthful, and forthright answers to questions about our organization, finances, and programs
- Be informed of our mission and the way we intend to use donated resources
- Request anonymity, as described above
- Expect that your relationship with AgroHouse Foundation is handled with respect and professionalism
