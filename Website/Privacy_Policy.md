# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

AgroHouse Foundation, a nonprofit organization ("we," "us," or "our"), operates [website URL] and our Facebook Page (collectively, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, interact with our Facebook Page, make a donation, or otherwise engage with our organization (e.g., volunteering, event registration, or program participation).

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Information You Provide to Us
We collect information you voluntarily give us, including:
- **Contact form submissions:** name, email address, phone number, and any message content you submit.
- **Email sign-ups:** your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or mailing list.
- **Donation information:** name, billing address, email, phone number, donation amount, and payment details when you make a donation to us. If your employer offers matching gifts, we may also collect employer information you provide for that purpose.
- **Payment information:** Donations are processed by a third-party payment processor (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Donorbox, Classy). **We do not store your full credit card number or payment credentials on our servers.** Our payment processor collects this information directly and is governed by its own privacy policy.
- **Volunteer and event information:** if you sign up to volunteer, attend an event, or apply for a program, we may collect name, contact information, availability, and other details relevant to that activity.

### b. Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:
- IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
- Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
- General location information (e.g., city/region, inferred from IP address)

### c. Information from Facebook
If you interact with our Facebook Page (e.g., messaging us, commenting, or following us), Facebook/Meta collects and processes data according to its own privacy policy. We may receive limited information from Facebook, such as your public profile name and any information you choose to share with us directly through Messenger or comments.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
- Send you requested information, newsletters, or updates about our mission and programs (you may unsubscribe at any time)
- Process donations, issue tax-deductible donation receipts, and maintain donor records
- Coordinate volunteer opportunities, event registration, and program participation
- Communicate with you about fundraising campaigns, events, or ways to get involved
- Improve our website and the effectiveness of our programs and outreach
- Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized transactions
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations (e.g., IRS reporting requirements for charitable donations)

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and **we do not share or sell our donor list to other organizations.** We may share your information with:
- **Payment processors** (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Donorbox) to complete donation transactions
- **Email service providers** (e.g., Mailchimp, Constant Contact) to manage newsletter and supporter communications
- **Facebook/Meta**, when you interact with our Facebook Page (subject to Meta's own privacy practices)
- **Matching gift or grant partners**, only if you voluntarily provide employer information for a matching gift program
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, if required by law, subpoena, or legal process (e.g., IRS reporting)
- **Service providers** who help us operate (e.g., web hosting, donor management software, analytics), under confidentiality obligations

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal information
- Opt out of marketing emails (via the "unsubscribe" link in any email)
- Request a copy of the data we hold about you

To exercise these rights, contact us at **Info@AgroHousefoundation.org**.

If you are located in the **European Union (EEA)**, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

If you are a **California resident**, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request deletion. We do not sell personal information.
