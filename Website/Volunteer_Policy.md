# Volunteer Policy

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

AgroHouse Foundation relies on the generosity and dedication of volunteers to support our mission and programs. This Volunteer Policy outlines what volunteers can expect from us, and what we expect from volunteers, to ensure a safe, respectful, and effective experience for everyone involved.

---

## 1. Purpose of This Policy

This policy applies to all individuals who volunteer their time, skills, or services to AgroHouse Foundation, whether on a one-time, occasional, or ongoing basis, and whether in person or remotely.

---

## 2. Volunteer Eligibility

- Volunteers must generally be at least [Insert Minimum Age, e.g., 16] years old, unless participating in a supervised family or group volunteer activity.
- Minors under [Insert Age] may be permitted to volunteer with written parental or guardian consent and, where required, direct adult supervision.
- Some roles may require a background check, reference check, or other screening, particularly where volunteers will work directly with vulnerable populations, children, or handle sensitive information.

---

## 3. Application and Onboarding

- Prospective volunteers may be asked to complete a volunteer application or interest form, which may include collecting name, contact information, availability, and relevant skills or experience.
- Volunteers may be required to attend an orientation or training session before beginning certain roles.
- AgroHouse Foundation reserves the right to decline or discontinue a volunteer placement at its discretion.

---

## 4. Volunteer Expectations

Volunteers are expected to:
- Act honestly, respectfully, and in a manner consistent with the mission and values of AgroHouse Foundation
- Follow reasonable instructions and guidelines provided by staff or volunteer coordinators
- Maintain confidentiality regarding sensitive information encountered while volunteering (e.g., donor information, beneficiary details, internal operations)
- Notify us as soon as possible if unable to fulfill a scheduled commitment
- Refrain from engaging in discriminatory, harassing, or unsafe behavior toward staff, other volunteers, beneficiaries, or the public
- Represent AgroHouse Foundation appropriately when identified as a volunteer, including on social media

---

## 5. What Volunteers Can Expect from Us

AgroHouse Foundation is committed to:
- Providing a safe and respectful volunteer environment
- Giving clear instructions, reasonable training, and necessary resources for assigned tasks
- Treating all volunteers fairly and without discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other protected status
- Recognizing and appreciating volunteer contributions
- Providing a point of contact for questions, concerns, or feedback

---

## 6. Health and Safety

Volunteers are expected to follow all health and safety guidelines provided for a given activity or event. Volunteers should immediately report any unsafe conditions, accidents, or injuries to their volunteer coordinator or a staff member. Depending on the activity, volunteers may be asked to sign a liability waiver prior to participation.

---

## 7. Confidentiality and Data Privacy

Volunteers may have access to confidential information, including donor records, beneficiary information, or internal organizational matters. Volunteers must not disclose such information outside the scope of their volunteer duties, both during and after their volunteer service. Any personal information collected from volunteers is handled in accordance with our **Privacy Policy**.

---

## 8. Termination of Volunteer Service

Either the volunteer or AgroHouse Foundation may end the volunteer relationship at any time, for any reason. We reserve the right to immediately remove a volunteer from a role or the organization in cases involving safety concerns, policy violations, or conduct inconsistent with our mission and values.

---

## 9. Non-Employment Relationship

Volunteering with AgroHouse Foundation does not create an employment relationship. Volunteers are not entitled to wages, benefits, or workers' compensation coverage unless otherwise required by applicable law.
